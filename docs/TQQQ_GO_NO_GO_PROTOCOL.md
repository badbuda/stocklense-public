# StockLens 8.0 — observed TQQQ graduation protocol

Evidence status: **RESEARCH / SHADOW ONLY. NOT AUTHORIZED FOR LIVE TRADING.**
Source of truth: `docs/backtest_live_readiness.json`, regenerated by
`model_readiness_audit.py`. Passing CI and beating QQQ historically do not
imply that live returns or risk are known.

## Canonical, and deliberately separate, evidence

| Evidence | What it can show | What it cannot show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Observed QQQ/TQQQ daily adjusted prices since TQQQ inception | Daily ETF path, leverage drag, cash-constrained whole-share allocation, cost/lag stresses | Historical 09:31 sell and 09:32 buy executions, exact exchange fills, actual tax, LEAN parity |
| Frozen StockLens 8.0 shadow signal | Decisions reproduced without hindsight after the completed QQQ bar | Orders were placed in an actual brokerage account |
| Prospective research-only SL9-007 challenger | Forward challenger observation after preregistration | Forward performance of frozen StockLens 8.0 brokerage portfolio |

## Release gates (all independently required)

1. **Observed TQQQ-only:** source metadata, overlap window and raw fingerprints
   are verified. No synthetic daily multiple, price interpolation or pre-inception returns.
2. **Data provenance:** store immutable raw QQQ/TQQQ daily OHLC snapshots;
   independently cross-check adjusted/unadjusted prices, distributions and splits
   against a second provider; identify the first time each daily bar was available.
3. **Trading-calendar and lookahead:** reproduce exactly which completed QQQ
   bar the frozen decision used. Inject future price perturbations: historical
   prior decisions must remain identical. Refuse stale or partial sessions.
4. **Equity/cash accounting:** reconcile positions, whole shares, available
   cash, dividend/split handling, buy/sell fees, slippage, deposits, and terminal
   account value. Drawdowns with deposits require a cashflow-neutral NAV index.
5. **Joint execution stress:** evaluate combined 0/1/2-session extra signal
   delays and 10/25/50 bps slippage, plus isolated single-axis ladders.
   All cases must preserve observed ETF inputs and identical QQQ comparator dates.
6. **Distribution and fragility:** disclose worst 21/63/252-session returns,
   overlapping 1/3/5-year rolling comparisons, negative calendar years,
   recovery duration, tail concentration and drawdowns. Do not choose a
   favorable window after seeing the outcome.
7. **Selection bias:** assemble the complete experimental search record, including
   all rejected variants, the count of tested hypotheses, reused holdouts and
   corrected multiple-testing results. Treat original development/validation/
   holdout that influenced 8.0 as selected evidence, not fresh validation.
8. **Original LEAN parity:** require independently sourced same-run timestamped
   daily holdings, NAV, cashflows, orders and fills, code commit, data fingerprints
   and precise rebalancing conventions. Missing exports are blockers, not zeroes.
9. **Forward frozen baseline:** retain decisions *before* market outcomes, capture
   at least 63 unique completed, paired paper sessions for an initial audit and
   252 for a longer review. These are evidence checkpoints, not proof or
   an automatic trigger to trade. Missed sessions stay recorded as missed.
10. **Paper-vs-replay tracking:** reconcile the same session and instrument,
    paid cashflows and NAV. Explain residual from price basis, minute execution,
    whole shares, uninvested cash, fees, spreads, dividends and provider changes.
11. **Broker sandbox:** test order-idempotency collisions, delayed acks, rejects,
    partial fills, reconnects, duplicate webhooks, account-position divergence,
    kill-switch, market closures, gap openings and disaster recovery. No real
    orders are authorized by the testing code.
12. **Risk/legal approval:** separately authorize position sizes, loss budgets,
    concentration and gap risk limits, emergency contacts, broker/tax eligibility
    and manual go/no-go decisions. No automatic scaling or model promotion.

## Automation boundaries

All unverified gates remain `BLOCKED`. No generator may synthesize paper
fills, forge a second-provider confirmation, silently mutate frozen rules,
promote SL9 challengers or submit live trades to resolve a blocker.
The current observed-ETF daily-OPEN replay is **not** true 09:31/09:32
execution parity. A 40% historical CAGR and 49% maximum drawdown can coexist
with devastating future losses. CI green means reproducible technical
operation, not validated investment profitability.
