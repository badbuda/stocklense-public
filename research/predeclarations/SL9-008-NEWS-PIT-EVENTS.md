# SL9-008 — News point-in-time historical event research, pre-registered 2026-10-10

**Hypothesis (NOT proven):** Explicitly timestamped news about policy announcements, macro surprises, geopolitical shocks, elections and corporate disclosures might explain subsequent ETF risk regimes or company stock return dispersion beyond frozen StockLens 8.0 price-only signals. This is an exploratory, separately versioned research layer, not a live trading rule.

## Four parallel, deliberately different research families

1. **Federal Reserve / CPI / jobs surprise:** FOMC statement and initial CPI/NFP print are outcomes only at their verified actual release UTC clock. Surprise = actual minus a CONSENSUS EXPECTATION snapshot collected before release. Announcement schedule known weeks before is *not* the decision outcome; realized rate level without rate-futures expectations is not a surprise.
2. **Conflict / elections / sanctions / tariffs:** normalize first genuinely observed vendor time, distinct news entities and repeated news stories; deduplicate syndication. Do not presume that war/election means price falls or that every GDELT co-occurrence is a newly initiated military event. Research tail dispersion and liquidity risk rather than hardwired directional signs.
3. **Earnings / M&A / guidance / regulation:** source SEC 8-K/10-Q/10-K and issuer disclosures with separately verified delivery timestamps. An 8-K is a filing, NOT automatically a takeover announcement; historical CIK-to-ticker mapping must be correct as of the event, including delisted firms.
4. **Headline coverage or market surprise spillovers:** use lagged normalized GDELT event coverage plus previously released ALFRED vintages, NOT today's revised macro observations. Compare whether any incremental feature improves detection of rare large daily/overnight losses beyond QQQ price, volatility and trend features.

## Strict causal and data contract

- Four clocks: event happened, press published, provider first monitored, vendor delivered, plus model processing buffer and order time. Historical orders only after first independently evidenced news availability. A market-moving 14:00 ET FOMC surprise cannot affect the previous 09:30 open.
- `news_event_pit.py`: source record SHA-256, original provider-record ID, canonical story key, UTC timestamp (no date-only), cryptographic receipt fingerprint and HTTPS archive provenance URL, source category, event kind and point-in-time ticker eligibility. An event captured and labeled TODAY must never be passed off as a 2015 contemporaneous generated signal. All fields are source metadata; no full article body in public repo.
- Scheduled announcements are eligible for *calendar event risk observation*, not unannounced content or future result. Modern GPT/LLM analyses of historical articles carry hindsight and possible training contamination; only frozen as-of classifier versions with no future leakage could be prospective signal candidates.
- Provider adapters in `news_provider_adapters.py` are conservative: GDELT 2.0 15-minute GKG source DATE is not independently proven earliest market availability; SEC acceptanceDateTime and form field alone do not establish a live broker-executable headline time or event semantic. They START UNVERIFIED. No automatic promotion from intake to PIT training.
- `news_archive_intake.py` can read private GDELT historical GKG 15-minute ZIP or a SEC public filings submissions JSON, or fetch one allowlisted bounded GDELT ZIP via HTTPS (owner command only; NO CI fetching). It emits only private unverified provider metadata JSONL, with both original raw source SHA and derived metadata SHA. Reconstruct historical receipt proof separately before promoting eligibility.
- `news_event_study.py` maps an evidentially eligible event to the first genuine XNYS session OPEN after archive delivery plus processing and 10 minutes. Compares ACTUAL observed adjusted QQQ/TQQQ open→1/5/21-day close log returns as descriptive labels only, grouping by fixed provider event categories. Missing market quote day, missing future horizon, cross-provider duplicate story, closed NYSE holiday or insufficient date precision never triggers fabricated performance.

## Research and investment gates

**Current verified source inventory:** GDELT 2.0 historically from Feb 2015, ALFRED vintage history does not guarantee intraday pre-open timestamp, FOMC original statements have time-of-release examples, BLS publication schedule uses ET release clocks, SEC EDGAR has public submissions/XBRL APIs but historical dissemination and company identity constraints remain. Provider sources are recorded at `research/news_sources_pit_registry.json`.

**What can be tested now:** code/source taxonomy, parser security, embargo / point-in-time session assignment, story dedup, known dates and calendar effects, first-seen/consensus readiness, fail-closed empty-cohort reporting. NO NEWS-ENHANCED CAGR has been computed or independently supported yet because no audited full historical provider first-seen dataset or expectation snapshots have been archived in the repository.

**Prospective frozen challenger gates, once genuine source data are available:** choose only one fixed news candidate before seeing outcomes. Compare against frozen 8.0 (and price-only matched challenger), by same execution engine, position notional, turnover, slippage 10/20/30bps, zero and one-day delayed vendor availability, max drawdown and geometric log wealth; include placebo 1-day event-time shifts, news clustering, exposure/vol matched event days, leave-one-crisis-out, country/company/time coverage changes and publisher survivorship.
