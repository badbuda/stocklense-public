## Claude hypotheses: coverage vs unresolved *data*, not just code

1. **Completed proxy falsifications:** caps/Kelly variants, slow ramps, EWMA5, real VIX prior close, downside/variance20, physical QLD, 70/15/15 & 85/7.5/7.5 GLD/TLT, current historical ML. There is no qualifying alpha. Do not search many more thresholds or declare 'VIX never works'; only these rules failed.
2. **Partially studied:** leverage-volatility drag day-by-day, dynamic re-entry, cash carry under EFFR effective dates, volatility/financing stress and daily-open cost sensitivity. The unresolved problem is execution *observability*: modeled NYSE daily open != achieved order fill, EFFR != cash deposit yield. Additional historical lag/cost grids cannot prove fill quality.
3. **Not empirically identified for 2010–2026:** real executable SPX/Nasdaq futures term structure, VVIX/SKEW historical first-available vintage, cross-market breadth with survivorship-safe constituent history, credit HY and rates with provider timing/revisions, pre-announcement CPI/FOMC *consensus vintage* and surprise, first-seen SEC/GDELT M&A/earnings/election/war archives, options surface/bid-ask, margin/broker rates, FX/tax-sensitive net results.
4. **Prospective-only candidate:** a direct continuously smoothed trend/SMA boundary or threshold hysteresis alternative remains distinct from the tested multi-day order ramp; require *new independently logged forward sessions* and prior immutable protocol, NOT re-optimizing on repeatedly reused historical dates.
